      The problem

      Your team is the integration layer.

      When orders, projects, documents and finances live in different files and inboxes, people end up moving information by hand. It works, until the business grows.

      See how VARKORA fixes it →
      01

      Re-typing the same data

      Orders, estimates and invoices get re-entered in three places, and every copy is a chance for an error.

      02

      Information everywhere

      Photos, assessments, shipping documents and approvals live in folders, phones and email threads nobody can search.

      03

      No one sees the whole picture

      Sales, operations and finance each keep their own version of the numbers.

      04

      Money stuck between steps

      Finished work and delivered orders wait to be billed because nobody saw they were done.

      None of this shows up as a line item. It shows up as slower jobs, thinner margins and a team that's always catching up.

      FAQ

      Questions teams ask us.

      Do we have to replace our current tools?

      Spreadsheets, scattered files and disconnected apps move into VARKORA, and we process them so they're ready on day one. Systems that should stay, like your ERP, accounting or e-commerce, we connect. You can export to Excel or CSV anytime.

      Does it work on my phone?

      Yes. VARKORA is designed mobile-first: you can see status, approve, get agent alerts and react from your phone, with the full detail on desktop.

      How long does it take?

      Discovery takes one to two weeks. Implementation depends on the solution and modules in scope, and you'll have a clear plan and timeline before you commit.

      How much does it cost?

      Discovery is a fixed price, credited to your project if you move forward. Implementation is priced by scope, followed by an annual subscription. Every proposal itemizes development, infrastructure, licenses, AI usage and support.

      Who owns the system and the data?

      VARKORA builds and maintains the platform and licenses it to your company. Your business data stays yours and can be exported at any time.

      Is our data secure?

      Yes. Access is role-based, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, every change is logged and backups run automatically.

      Do you use AI?

      Yes, inside the platform: agents and automations that draft, check and flag work, always with permissions and human review. You decide what they can do.

      What happens after launch?

      Your subscription includes support, updates and improvements, and you can add modules as you grow.

      Do you work outside the U.S.?

      Yes. We focus on U.S. companies and work with teams in other countries too, including cross-border operations.
